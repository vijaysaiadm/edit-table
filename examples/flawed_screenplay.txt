KALYANAM — A FLAWED SAMPLE SCREENPLAY (FOR TESTING edit-table)

Written by

Sample Script QA Team

NOTE TO TESTERS: This script deliberately contains known defects so you can verify
the analysis pipeline catches them. Planted flaws are listed at the end.

EXT. VILLAGE FAIR - DAY

A festival crowd. KALYAN, 28, charming but aimless, helps his father SESHU, 60s, at a flower stall. They argue about Kalyan's future.

SESHU
Your brother runs the shop in the city. Why can't you be like him?

KALYAN
Because I am not him, Nanna.

EXT. BUS STOP - DAY

Kalyan waits with a suitcase. His mother LAKSHMI, 50s, packs him tiffin and cries a little.

LAKSHMI
Call when you reach.

Kalyan nods. The bus arrives.

INT. BUS - DAY

Kalyan watches the village go by through the window, thinking.

EXT. CITY BUS STATION - DAY

Kalyan arrives, overwhelmed by the noise. He asks directions from a stranger.

INT. BROTHER'S SHOP - DAY

Kalyan's brother PRAVEEN, 35, busy and sharp, barely looks up.

PRAVEEN
You can sleep in the back room. But I need you at the counter from morning.

Kalyan agrees quietly.

INT. SHOP BACK ROOM - NIGHT

Kalyan unpacks. He finds an OLD BRASS KEY in his bag — his grandmother gave it to him years ago, he remembers suddenly. He looks at it, puzzled, then puts it in his pocket. (SETUP — never paid off.)

INT. SHOP - DAY (MONTAGE)

Days pass. Kalyan learns the trade. Customers come and go. Praveen corrects his mistakes. The routine settles.

EXT. TEA STALL - EVENING

Kalyan meets MEENA, 26, bold and funny, a regular at the stall. They banter.

MEENA
You always order the same tea. Ever tried the lemon one?

KALYAN
Why change what works?

They laugh. (BEAT 1 of the romance — will be repeated twice more with no evolution.)

INT. SHOP - DAY

A supplier short-changes Praveen. Kalyan notices but says nothing.

INT. TEA STALL - EVENING

Kalyan and Meena again. Same seats.

MEENA
You always order the same tea. Ever tried the lemon one?

KALYAN
Why change what works?

They laugh. (BEAT 2 — identical dialogue, repeated emotional beat.)

INT. SHOP - DAY

The supplier cheats Praveen again, bigger this time. Kalyan again says nothing.

EXT. MARKET STREET - DAY

Kalyan follows the supplier and discovers he is diverting stock to a rival shop run by DURGA, 40s, cold and calculating.

INT. DURGA'S SHOP - DAY

Durga catches Kalyan snooping. Her men warn him off. She smiles.

DURGA
Village boys should stay in the village.

INT. TEA STALL - EVENING

Kalyan tells Meena everything. She urges him to tell Praveen.

MEENA
This is your family. You have to speak.

KALYAN
If I speak and I'm wrong, Praveen will never trust me.

INT. SHOP - NIGHT

INTERVAL BLOCK: Praveen counts the day's earnings. They are short again. Kalyan almost speaks, then doesn't. Praveen looks at him, suspicious. NOTHING ELSE HAPPENS. (FLAT INTERVAL — no reveal, no escalation, no new question.)

INT. SHOP - DAY

Praveen confronts Kalyan about the missing stock and accuses him of theft. Kalyan, hurt, finally reveals the supplier and Durga.

INT. DURGA'S SHOP - DAY

Kalyan and Praveen confront Durga with proof. She dismisses them.

DURGA
Paper doesn't scare me.

INT. SHOP - NIGHT

The brothers argue. Praveen feels betrayed that Kalyan hid it so long. Lakshmi calls; Kalyan lies that everything is fine. (KARUNA beat.)

INT. TEA STALL - EVENING

Meena comforts Kalyan. (BEAT 3 — the same comfort-banter rhythm as beats 1 and 2; the romance has not evolved.)

MEENA
You always order the same tea.

KALYAN
Some things don't need to change.

INT. WAREHOUSE - NIGHT

Kalyan sneaks in and photographs the diverted stock. He is caught. A chase. He escapes with the photos but drops his phone. (New question raised: will Durga find the phone? — never answered. BROKEN PROMISE.)

EXT. CITY BUS STATION - DAY

Kalyan decides to leave the city, defeated. He says goodbye to Meena.

MEENA
So that's it? You run?

INT. SHOP - DAY

Praveen, alone, realizes the accounts finally add up only if Kalyan was telling the truth. He rushes to the station.

EXT. BUS STATION - DAY

Praveen stops the bus. Brothers reconcile in front of everyone. Durga is arrested off-screen — we only hear about it from a bystander's radio. (CLIMAX RESOLVED BY EXPosition — the villain's defeat is told, not shown.)

EXT. VILLAGE FAIR - DAY

Kalyan returns home, now confident, and reopens his father's stall with new ideas. He hugs Seshu. LAKSHMI smiles. (THE BRASS KEY from scene 9 is never mentioned again. UNPAID SETUP.)

FADE OUT.

PLANTED FLAWS (what the pipeline should catch):
1. Three consecutive setup scenes with no escalation (Scenes 2-4: bus stop, bus, station).
2. UNPAID SETUP: the brass key (Scene 9) is never explained or used.
3. BROKEN PROMISE: the dropped phone in Scene 20 — raised, never resolved.
4. Romance repetition: the same tea joke / emotional beat three times (Scenes 10, 13, 22) with no evolution.
5. FLAT INTERVAL: Scene 16 raises suspicion but no reveal, escalation, or new question.
6. CLIMAX TOLD NOT SHOWN: Durga's defeat happens off-screen (Scene 25).
7. PACING: protagonist is passive for the entire first half (Scenes 12, 14 — sees the cheating, does nothing, twice).
