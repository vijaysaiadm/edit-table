A Sample Screenplay for Testing madhav-edit

EXT. CITY MARKET - MORNING

The market wakes. Vendors shout. RAJU, 30s, sharp-eyed, pushes through the crowd, late for something. He checks his phone: a missed call from ANAND. He pockets it and hurries on.

INT. POLICE STATION - DAY

INSPECTOR VIJAY, 50s, reads a file with growing concern. A young constable waits.

VIJAY
The witness from the market. Find him before they do.

The constable nods and exits.

EXT. RIVERSIDE - EVENING

Raju meets LEELA, 20s, quick-witted and worried. They keep their voices low.

LEELA
You saw the number plate. That is why they are following you.

RAJU
Then we go to the Inspector before sunrise.

INT. RAJU'S FLAT - NIGHT

A knock. Raju opens the door — no one. On the floor: a photograph of Leela, crossed out in red. Raju stares, fear turning to resolve.

EXT. WAREHOUSE DISTRICT - NIGHT

Raju moves between containers, flashlight off. A FIGURE steps out: the DRIVER from the market. A fight — short, brutal. Raju wins, barely, and finds the ledger he was never supposed to see.

INT. POLICE STATION - PRE-DAWN

Raju lays the ledger on Vijay's desk.

RAJU
Names, routes, payments. Everything.

Vijay studies it, then looks up.

VIJAY
Now they will come for both of us.

EXT. CITY MARKET - DAWN

The market wakes again — same shots, calmer now. Raju and Leela walk through it, unafraid this time. Vijay watches from a distance, and allows himself half a smile.
